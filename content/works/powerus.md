Powerus is a Y Combinator–backed startup building Germany's leading career platform for skilled trade professionals — think electricians, plumbers, mechanics, carpenters, and more. With over 200,000 registered workers, the platform matches candidates with permanent jobs based on their skills, location, and preferences, letting them apply with a single click.

I work in the **Worker Squad**, the team responsible for the entire B2C experience: the web and mobile platform that workers use to discover jobs, build their profiles, and manage applications. My day-to-day involves shipping features across the full stack — **Angular** on the frontend and **NestJS** on the backend, backed by **MongoDB** — partnering closely with product and design to drive engagement and performance.

Beyond product work, I'm part of the **Platform Guild**, where I contribute to infrastructure and developer experience: managing deployments with **Kubernetes** and **Helm**, working with **AWS** services, and keeping our systems reliable as we scale. I also take an active role in our internal guilds, leading initiatives that improve code quality, maintainability, and team productivity.
