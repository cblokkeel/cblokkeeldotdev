Powerus is Germany's largest job platform built specifically for skilled trades and blue-collar workers — think electricians, plumbers, mechanics, carpenters, and more. With over 200,000 registered workers and thousands of open positions, the platform matches candidates with permanent jobs based on their skills, location, and preferences, letting them apply with a single click and no cover letter.

I joined the **Worker Squad**, the team responsible for the entire B2C experience: the web app and mobile experience that workers use to discover jobs, build their profiles, and manage applications. My day-to-day involves building features across the full stack — **Angular** on the frontend and **NestJS** on the backend, backed by **MongoDB**.

Beyond feature work, I'm also part of the **Platform Guild**, which means I regularly contribute to infrastructure and developer experience: managing deployments with **Kubernetes** and **Helm**, working with **AWS** services, and making sure our systems stay reliable as the platform grows.
